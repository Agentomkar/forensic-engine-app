Privacy Policy

FEIA Scam Check (Android app) · Effective 28 October 2026

FEIA Scam Check helps you decide whether a link, SMS, WhatsApp message or email is a phishing or fraud attempt. This page explains exactly what happens to what you submit.

What the app collects

Only the content you choose to scan: a link, a message you paste or share into the app, or an email file (.eml) you pick. The app does not read your SMS inbox, contacts, call logs, location, browsing history, photos or accounts, and it requests no Android permissions other than internet access.

How it is used

Your submission is sent over HTTPS to our analysis server, examined by the FEIA engine (machine-learning classifiers and rule-based forensic checks), and the verdict is returned to your phone. Submissions are processed in memory and are not stored on the server, not added to any database, and not used to train models.

If the server operator has enabled VirusTotal reputation lookups, links found in your submission (not the surrounding message text) are sent to VirusTotal to check their reputation, under VirusTotal's terms.

Our hosting provider keeps standard request logs (IP address, time, request path) for a limited period for security and abuse prevention. These logs do not contain the text you submit.

On your device

Your last 20 scan results are kept on your phone only, so you can review them. You can delete them at any time from the History screen, or by uninstalling the app.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with advertisers or data brokers. The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Data deletion

Because submissions are not stored on the server, there is no server-side account or record to delete. On-device history is deleted from the History screen. For any question, email the developer at the address below.

Not a substitute for official help

Verdicts are automated assessments and can be wrong. If you have lost money, report it immediately at cybercrime.gov.in or call the national helpline 1930.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
