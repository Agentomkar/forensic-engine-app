          About this app

          FEIA Scam Check is the phone client of the Forensic Evidence Intelligence Agent, a cybersecurity project that investigates links, messages and emails with machine-learning classifiers and forensic checks such as SPF, DKIM and sender spoofing.

          Results are automated and can be wrong. The app never reads your SMS inbox, contacts or accounts. It only sees what you choose to check.
